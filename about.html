Who we are

Beauté & Bien-être, done right

Frasco Beauty began with a frustration every beauty lover in Morocco knows too well — finding authentic products at fair prices, without the worry of fakes. We set out to change that.

Today we are a premium boutique delivering across the whole country. From iconic luxury perfumes like Bvlgari to carefully selected skin, body, hair and dental care, every item is verified genuine and sourced from trusted partners.

Our community of more than 118,000 followers trusts us daily for curated picks, honest advice and real deals. That trust is the most valuable thing we own — and we protect it with every single order.
